INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Robust higher exposure means the dimension is higher in every eligible peer group represented for that country.
Robust non-higher exposure means the dimension is never higher in the eligible peer groups represented.
Peer-sensitive exposure means the higher/non-higher classification changes across eligible peer groups.
Insufficient peer coverage means fewer than two peer groups classify that country-dimension.
Two-group and three-group evidence are both retained, but their coverage strength is explicitly recorded.
Three-class migration may occur without a change in higher-exposure activation.
The tested peer groups overlap and are not independent replications.
These labels describe peer-relative classification stability, not absolute risk, crisis probability, default probability or predictive accuracy.
No composite risk score is calculated.
