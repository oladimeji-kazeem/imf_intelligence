METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mean and median growth are descriptive historical measures, not forecasts.
Standard deviation measures dispersion and is sensitive to extreme observations.
Median absolute deviation and interquartile range provide robust dispersion measures.
Contraction frequency is based on negative annual real GDP growth, not recession dating.
Recovery means the first subsequent positive annual growth observation within five calendar years. It does not mean real GDP has regained its pre-contraction level.
Rolling five-year statistics require five consecutive numeric annual observations.
Ireland's exceptional 2015 growth is retained. It should not be interpreted as ordinary domestic economic growth.
No arbitrary risk thresholds, composite scores, causal claims or forecasts are generated.
