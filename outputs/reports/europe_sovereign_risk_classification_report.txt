CLASSIFICATION METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Classifications are peer-relative terciles among available countries.
They are not absolute risk thresholds, credit ratings or default probabilities.
Higher debt, debt increases and positive debt-increasing contributions represent higher exposure.
For primary balance, a lower value represents greater fiscal pressure.
Accounting adjustments are exposures, not causal estimates.
Five-year cumulative accounting components are classified only when all five annual observations are available.

INTERPRETATION LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Peer-relative terciles do not establish absolute risk.
2. The peer group contains 17 European countries, not all European sovereigns.
3. Debt burden uses the 2024 debt-to-GDP observation.
4. Debt trajectory uses five annual debt changes from 2020-2024.
5. Primary balance is not cyclically adjusted.
6. Interest-growth contribution uses the opening-debt-implied interest-rate proxy.
7. Financial-asset acquisition can increase gross debt while adding assets.
8. SFA and ORADJ components are accounting adjustments, not causal estimates.
9. Incomplete five-year component histories are not treated as complete cumulative totals.
10. Accounting coverage grades measure coverage, not source accuracy.
11. A missing component is not interpreted as zero.
12. No composite sovereign risk score or default probability is calculated.
