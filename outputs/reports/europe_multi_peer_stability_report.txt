METHODOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Consistent higher exposure means all observed eligible peer groups classify the dimension as higher.
2. Consistent non-higher exposure means no observed eligible peer group classifies the dimension as higher.
3. Peer-sensitive exposure means higher/non-higher activation changes across observed groups.
4. Classification migration separately captures changes among higher, moderate and lower labels.
5. Countries appearing in fewer than two tested groups are marked insufficient for cross-peer stability assessment.
6. These are descriptive stability labels, not confidence intervals, probabilities or absolute risk ratings.
7. The three peer groups overlap and are not independent samples.
8. Consistent non-higher exposure does not mean the country has no economic vulnerability.
9. No composite score, crisis probability or default probability is estimated.
