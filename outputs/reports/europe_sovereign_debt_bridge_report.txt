METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The master panel is based on the 85 Eurostat interest-growth observations.
2. SFA components are missing for some country-years; missing values are not zero.
3. MIO_NAC values are converted using current-year nominal GDP.
4. The primary balance is observed and not cyclically adjusted.
5. The interest-growth rate is an opening-debt-implied proxy, not a separately sourced market yield.
6. SFA and ORADJ components are accounting contributions, not causal estimates.
7. The integrated bridge does not establish that a component was caused by fiscal policy.
8. Country cumulative totals should only be compared over common observation windows.
9. The detailed ORADJ categories are components of ORADJ and must not be added to ORADJ again.
10. A small reconciliation residual does not independently validate every source definition.
