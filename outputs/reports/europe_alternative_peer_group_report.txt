METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Peer-group definitions are explicit project choices.
2. The Northern/Western group is not an official classification.
3. Small regional groups are not assigned tercile classifications.
4. Overlapping groups are not independent validation samples.
5. Group comparisons use the same raw measurements and direction registry.
6. Changes reflect peer composition, not changes in economic conditions.
7. Tercile thresholds are descriptive, not calibrated risk thresholds.
8. The analysis does not estimate absolute risk or crisis probability.
9. Results do not establish predictive accuracy.

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
A low classification-change rate indicates consistency with the 20-country baseline under that alternative peer definition.
A high rate indicates sensitivity to peer-group composition. It does not establish that either classification is economically correct.
