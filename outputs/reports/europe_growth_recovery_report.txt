METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Consecutive negative annual growth observations are grouped into contraction episodes.
The real GDP index is chained from observed annual real GDP growth rates, using 100 as the starting value within each continuous data segment.
Recovery occurs when the chained index returns to or exceeds its level immediately before the contraction episode.
A missing observation or calendar-year gap breaks the index chain. Recovery cannot be established across that gap.
Episodes without a preceding baseline are unassessable.
This is an annual GDP-level recovery measure, not an official recession dating methodology.
The chained index is a diagnostic reconstruction from annual growth rates, not a substitute for official real GDP volume levels.
