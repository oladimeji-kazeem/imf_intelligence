METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Relative positions are calculated within identical reference years.
2. Terciles are only calculated when a comparison group contains at least 5 countries.
3. A relative weakness does not establish absolute economic distress.
4. Current account and trade growth are contextual indicators, not automatically good or bad.
5. Higher historical dispersion is not proof of economic vulnerability.
6. Historical sample windows differ between countries and indicators.
7. Latest historical observations are not real-time economic conditions.
8. No composite economic score or sovereign credit rating is calculated.
